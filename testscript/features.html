01 / RUNTIME SUBSYSTEMS

Interactive 5-Layer Explorer

Click or ask the voice assistant to swap architectural layers

src/sdk/live-client.js • 16kHz In / 24kHz Out

Bidirectional PCM Streaming & Sub-50ms Barge-In

Microphone audio is captured inside a dedicated AudioWorkletProcessor, converted to 16-bit PCM, and streamed to relay.deploymate.in. Downstream 24kHz PCM chunks are decoded into Float32 AudioBuffer nodes and scheduled gaplessly along nextPlayTime.

src/sdk/ghost-cursor.js • Shadow DOM + 60fps rAF

Isolated ShadowRoot Overlay, Viewport Centering & Fitts's Law Physics

Mounted inside an open ShadowRoot on document.documentElement so client CSS never leaks in. Before flying to a target, the engine smoothly centers the actual component in the usable viewport, waits for scroll velocity to settle to zero, and glides along a Cubic Bézier curve.

src/sdk/dom-scanner.js • Light DOM + Shadow Piercing

Persistent `data-wo-id` Tagging & Substantive Component Resolution

Every interactive node receives a deterministic data-wo-id="wo-N" attribute and can be resolved by CSS selector, ID, or accessible label. Landmark section selectors automatically resolve past title headers directly onto the actual table, tablist, carousel, or form.

src/sdk/ghost-cursor.js • React/Vue Setters + Pointer Gestures

Universal Form Co-Pilot & Multi-Modal Gesture Synthesis

Normalizes spoken email addresses ("ajay at deploymate dot in" → ajay@deploymate.in), invokes native HTMLInputElement.prototype.value setters for React/Vue state compatibility, selects dropdown options, interpolates range sliders, and renders dashed SVG swipe trails.

src/relay/weborient_relay.py • Multi-Tenant Auth & Credit Metering

Origin Verification, 30s Credit Ticks & Cross-Page Session Handoff

Validates the customer's data-site-key against registered domains via the HTTP Origin header, deducts voice credits every 30 seconds of active streaming, and persists non-linear To-Do progress across page redirects and hard refreshes.
